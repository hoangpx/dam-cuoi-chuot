Chương VI · Tìm Chuột (story, owner: chuột mẹ calls her children home from the fields; the found ones are child mice, mẹ chuột stands by the strip of heads, the intro reads "Gọi N con về", a win "ĐỦ CẢ!") (js/ch6/, SAVE6 {done[], tut, dotted} in localStorage 'dcc.c6', song SONGS[7]; chapter id 6 because V is the brood's adventures opened by chương IV). It replaced the board-game chapter Cờ Đất (owner: "bỏ cái cờ"; Kẻ Tam Giác is parked in js/parked/). Meowdoku with mice: one mouse per row, column and coloured plot, none touching even diagonally. LEVELS like chương II: an album grouped by size (Tập chơi, Ruộng 5×5 … 10×10), each card opened by finishing the one before; level 1 = the guided 4×4 practice field (MD_TUT steps: dim all but the cells to act on, a slip above with the rule and one below with what to do, a finger showing tap / double tap / swipe; only lit cells answer; no hearts lost). The rest come from js/ch6/levels.js, made OFFLINE by  (each size cached in tools/mice-cache/N.json, then levels.js is rebuilt from every cache): random placement + plots grown from each mouse, plot borders nudged until the answer is unique, kept only if a logic solver (no guessing) finishes it, graded by the hardest rule needed (1 single place left, 2 a group confined to one line/plot, 3 one-step what-if, 4 k plots in k lines) and how often; within a size the levels run easiest → hardest. SCREEN (owner's reference): back button + "Màn N" + hearts on top, three little rule cards (mini 3×3 fields), a strip of mouse heads (one per plot colour, filled in when found), the field in a rounded panel of rounded cells. INPUT: one tap = a white × ("no mouse here"), delayed by the double-tap time (MD_DBL 300 ms) so a double tap never flashes one; a double tap = a mouse; a drag marks many (starting on a mark wipes); a wrong mouse costs one of 3 hearts and a red × (white-edged) stays there for good. Level 2 hatches ruled-out cells and shows the drag with a finger until the player has dragged once. "Tìm N con chuột" card before each field. After a level a tap: win → next level, loss → the same field again.
