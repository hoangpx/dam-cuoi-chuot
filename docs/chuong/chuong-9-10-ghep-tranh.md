Chương IX · Ghép Tranh 拼圖 (owner, 2026-10-20; branch chuong-9-ghep-tranh, js/ghep/manh.js + game.js, SAVE9 in localStorage dcc.c9, registerChapter id 9, mode c9play): jigsaw puzzles cut from the REAL Đông Hồ prints already in img/ch3 (the five of chương III). 15 levels in three tiers, five pictures each: about 12 pieces upright, about 24 and about 40 turned at random; each opens after the one before. Real jigsaw pieces (tabs and blanks, seeded per level, c9Edge/c9PiecePath, each piece baked into its own canvas); a pale ghost and cut lines on the board, a table for the loose pieces, an eye button to peek at the whole picture, a clock, a finished print and confetti. POINTER RULES: mouse: press lifts (drag, or click and it follows the mouse until the next click), right click / wheel / R turns it a quarter clockwise; touch: touching lifts, drag it or tap elsewhere to put it there, a quick vertical flick ON the piece turns it (down clockwise, up anticlockwise; the piece goes back to where it was) and a small round arrow beside the lifted piece does the same. A piece dropped within .45 of a cell near its place and upright snaps and stays. To add pictures: add an entry to C9_PICS (src, w, h, names), put the file in img/ch9/ or img/ch3/. All checked with synthetic pointer events (mouse click-follow-drop, right click, touch flick up/down, slow drag, tap-to-place) and a forced win; not played on a real phone yet.

Chương IX v2 (owner, 2026-10-20: no faded guide, any angle, join anywhere, thick cardboard): the board and the faded print are GONE (only the eye button shows the sample for 4 s, tap again to hide). Pieces live in GROUPS with one rigid pose (lattice origin on the screen + angle): two groups JOIN on drop when a piece of one touches a neighbour of the other in the print and the angle differs by ≤ 14° and the lattice origins by ≤ 0.38 of the smaller cell side; the smaller snaps to the bigger and the two pieces flash once (flash ring and a white wash). A joined part is lifted, moved and turned as ONE piece, anywhere on the screen; the print is done when all pieces are one group (then the finished print is shown big). Thick cardboard: each piece is baked twice (printed face with a bevel, kraft silhouette); the sides are a stack of silhouette copies offset to the lower right with a shadow, deeper while lifted. 35–40 pieces draw in about 1 ms a frame. Levels: tiers differ only in piece count (about 12, 24, 40).
