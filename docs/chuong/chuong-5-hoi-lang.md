# Chương V · Hội Làng (phòng chơi chung, js/hoilang + server/phong-choi)

Ghi chú theo thứ tự thời gian (cũ trước, mới sau; mục sau có thể sửa mục trước). Tách từ CLAUDE.md ngày 2026-10-22.

Chương V · Hội Làng 會村 (owner, 2026-10-19; branch phong-choi-chung; it took the place of the locked placeholder "Bầy Chuột Phiêu Lưu" (removed from js/ch4/cho.js; owner 2026-10-19: "thay bằng chương V") and replaced the dropped hide-and-seek idea, whose work stays on branch chuong-tron-tim): ONE shared room that runs all day. Everyone who opens the chapter sits in one grid (a column count that fits the screen, all players visible), plays the round in progress or watches and is seated at the next. Server = a Cloudflare Durable Object (server/phong-choi, README there; deployed to https://dcc-phong-choi.dcc-phong-choi.workers.dev, account of the owner's Cloudflare; Node was installed unzipped in C:\Users\Hoang\tools\node for wrangler; redeploy = `npx wrangler deploy`): it keeps the clock, the schedule, the bots and the results; browsers draw and send "I pressed at server-time X". Cycle: announce 4 s (seats locked) → play → result 9 s → next, games alternate 'ten' / 'pomelo'; when the room is empty the loop stops. Fewer than 6 people → bots (named "(máy)") fill, so it always plays; the prize is 10 đồng × everyone in the round (bots count) to the winner only, paid into SAVE4.money by the client on the server's result (HL.paid guards a double pay). Games: (1) 'ten': the clock shows 0..3 s then is hidden; press when 10,000 s is up; nearest wins; (2) 'pomelo': each player's pomelo falls on their head as (t / fall time)², fall time 2.0–3.5 s per player; press stops it; the smallest gap without being hit wins (late = hit = lose); everyone sees everyone's pomelo stop live because clients animate from t0 + each fall time and only the 'press' events travel. Fairness: the browser syncs a clock to the server (HL_NET.now(), lowest-RTT sample of 8, resynced every 12 s), presses carry the estimated server time; the server accepts it only within the measured latency (c.rtt/2 + 150 ms), else it uses its own arrival time minus half the RTT; a cheater can still shave a little, acceptable for in-game money. Bots decide their press times when the round is created. Origin allowed: damcuoichuot.com, localhost/LAN. Files: js/hoilang/net.js (HL_NET: socket, clock, reconnect), js/hoilang/game.js (shell, grid, both games, chapter id 5, save dcc.hl {sort, rounds, wins, earned}); the player's look is a random adult mouse sort kept per device, the name is the profile's. Cost (2026-10): Workers Free covers a small crowd; the paid plan is about 5 USD a month if it grows. Next games for the series: the owner will add more; the server's `game` field and hlInfo / hlCell are where a new one plugs in.

## Trò mới (2026-10-22): Đếm chuột trong hang, Nhớ tranh Đông Hồ, Ngoáy tai mèo, Mê cung, Thái hành tây

Code: server/phong-choi/src/index.js (setupNew / actNew / gameTick / rows / winnersOf; id in GAMES and NEWG), drawing in js/hoilang/tro2.js (HL_G.<id>), id also in HL_GAMES (js/hoilang/game.js); test.mjs has a bot for each.

**Before a deploy:** `cd server/phong-choi && npx wrangler deploy`, and bump the Room name (the `idFromName` places) because the
protocol changed (new messages `mzp`, `mzv`, `onv`; `act` of Đếm chuột now carries the turn as `tn`, not `t`: a field `t` replaced the message type `act`,
which is why nobody's guess ever arrived before). Bump `VERSION` in index.html (it is 2026-10-28u). Run `node tools/offline-list.js` after adding images.

- **Đếm chuột (`dem`)**: 5 turns of 2–3 / 3–6 / 3–8 (2–3 white) / 3–8 (the cat may cross) / 3–8 (two herds from opposite sides, the cat may cross) mice, never the same count twice running.
  The number on the dial is a running total, sent by itself 450 ms before the turn's input ends (`tick` hook in `updateHL`, no button). Only an exact total wins (equal totals share the pot); nobody exact = nobody paid. Mice are drawn in code (`demDrawSil`, black or white with an ink edge), the cat `demDrawCat`.
- **Đếm chuột trong hang (`dhang`)**: three upright two-legged mice (`demDrawUp`) are covered by a burrow, then mice go in / out in four steps (1 s apart); count what is left inside. Exact only, as above.
- **Nhớ tranh Đông Hồ (`nhotranh`)**: 10 turns, tap a Start button (auto-start at 12 s), 30 s from Start. Turn t shows a print on top and 2 / 4 / 6 prints below, one of them the print of turn t−1. Only all-correct players win, quickest takes the pot. 12 prints in `HL_NT_PICS` (js/hoilang/tro2.js) — keep `NP` in the server equal to its length. The player's own prints live in `img/hoilang/`.
- **Ngoáy tai mèo (`ngoay`)**: hold to push the stick along an ear canal, let go to pull it out with the pellets it passed; the eardrum is at depth 1, the last pellet at 0.955. 6–8 pellets and a random stick speed each game. The inside is shown for 4.5 s, then the face covers it once the stick passes the first pellet (memory game). Most pellets wins, equal numbers share; pierced = 0. 15 s to start. The cry is the sample `meovo` of chương I tranh 6 (`MEOW_B64`). The server tells the others nothing but "done" (a depth would give the eardrum away).
- **Mê cung (`mecung`)**: a perfect 17×17 maze, everyone starts in the middle, one gap in the outer wall at one of the three farthest border cells. The server sends each player only the 13×13 cells around them (`mzv`), never the whole maze; the page shows 9×9 cells on every screen. Arrow keys work. First out wins, 60 s, nobody out = nobody paid. Tested with a depth-first bot (`test.mjs`), 23–44 s.
- **Thái hành tây (`thaihanh`)**: each tap is a slice of 40–60; a tap after the onion is gone cuts the hand and loses (the cry grows and shakes). First done and unhurt wins; the round ends 1.5 s after the first unhurt finisher, 2.5 s after everybody is cut, or at 30 s. The face cries only after the first slice.

Local testing: `npx wrangler dev --port 8788 --var ONLY_GAME:<id>` in `server/phong-choi`, serve the repo folder, open `/index.html?hlws=ws://localhost:8788/ws`;
`DEVPFX=realp node test.mjs <players> <rounds> ws://127.0.0.1:8788/ws` plays bots. A local `wrangler dev` sometimes hangs after being restarted: kill every `workerd` and `node` process first.
